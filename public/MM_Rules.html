4. General Rules
  • Originality: The project must contain a personalized contribution by the team. Fully copied projects are not allowed.
  • Technology: Teams may use any programming language, framework, tool or technology.
  • Devices: Teams must bring their own laptops and required software, libraries, APIs, datasets, etc. for Round 2.
  • Mentoring: Mentors may guide, suggest or provide coding support in Round 1. No mentor assistance is allowed in Round 2.
  • Disqualification: Projects found to be 100% plagiarized/copied will be disqualified.
  • Final Submission: After Round 2, teams must submit the updated code/demo link and a brief explanation within the given deadline in the same project folder.
